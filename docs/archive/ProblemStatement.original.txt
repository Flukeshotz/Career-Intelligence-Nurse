MVP PROBLEM STATEMENT
Personalized Career Intelligence for Indian Nurses

1. The One-Line Problem
Indian nurses have to piece together jobs, government vacancies, exams, deadlines, results and career opportunities from many fragmented sources, while having no simple way to understand which opportunities are actually relevant to them or what they should do next.
The opportunity is to test whether a nurse-specific, personalized information and opportunity layer can replace this fragmented behaviour.

2. The Bigger Product Vision
The long-term vision is not simply:
“A website with nursing jobs.”
And it is not:
“An app containing everything related to nursing.”
The long-term vision is:
A career intelligence system for nurses.

And combine the two:
ME + MARKET = WHAT SHOULD I DO NEXT?

3. The Core Insight Behind the Product
The market already contains many products solving individual parts of the problem.
Nursing exam platforms help nurses prepare.
Job platforms help nurses find jobs.
Government websites publish notifications.
Telegram and WhatsApp distribute alerts.
YouTube explains complicated processes.
Recruiters help with overseas opportunities.
Google helps users find all of the above.
Therefore, the problem is not:
“There is no information for nurses.”
The problem is:
“The information is fragmented, difficult to interpret, frequently changing, and not personalized to the individual nurse.”
Today, the nurse herself has to become the integration layer.

5. The Fundamental User Problem
This fragmented behaviour creates five problems.
Problem 1 — Discovery
“How do I know what opportunities exist?”
There are too many sources.

Problem 2 — Relevance
“Which of these opportunities actually apply to me?”
Most platforms show opportunities without deeply understanding:
qualification
experience
specialization
registration
location
career goal

Problem 3 — Understanding
“What does this notification actually mean?”
Government and overseas information can be difficult to interpret.
Users often need explanations such as:
“Can I apply?”
“What documents do I need?”
“Do I need experience?”
“What happens after I apply?”

Problem 4 — Timing
“When do I need to act?”
Applications, deadlines, admit cards, exams and results change continuously.
Missing one date can mean missing an opportunity.

Problem 5 — Decision-making
“Given all these options, what should I do?”
This is the biggest long-term whitespace.
Existing platforms largely show information.
The eventual product should help the nurse make a decision.

6. The Strategic Whitespace
The competitive whitespace is:
Personalized nursing career intelligence
More specifically:
A system that understands the nurse, continuously tracks the nursing opportunity market, filters opportunities based on the nurse’s profile, explains what matters, and eventually recommends the next best action.

7. The Primary MVP Problem
The MVP should focus on the first part of this vision.
Indian nurses do not have a simple, trusted and personalized place to discover relevant nursing jobs and government recruitment opportunities, understand the key requirements and deadlines, and stay informed when important information changes.
Therefore:
The MVP’s core question is:
“What nursing opportunities are relevant to me right now?”

8. Primary Job-to-be-Done
When I am looking for my next nursing opportunity, help me discover relevant jobs and government recruitment opportunities, quickly understand whether I can apply, know what I need to do and make sure I don’t miss important deadlines or updates.
Secondary JTBD:
Keep me informed about important changes in the nursing job and recruitment market without requiring me to repeatedly search multiple websites.

13. Trust Is Not a Feature — It Is a Requirement
Because the product aggregates information, accuracy is critical.
Every important opportunity should show:
SOURCE
Where did this information come from?
OFFICIAL LINK
Where can I verify or apply?
LAST VERIFIED
When was this checked?
STATUS
Open / Upcoming / Closed / Result Released / Expired
SOURCE TYPE
Official / Verified secondary / Other
The product should never hide the original source.
The philosophy should be:
We make the information easier to understand and discover. The original authority remains visible.

14. Scraping Strategy
The product may use publicly available information, feeds, APIs, structured extraction or other mechanisms to discover opportunities.
However:
Scraping is not the product.
The product is:
Discover → structure → verify → personalize → alert.
Scraping is simply one potential input mechanism.
The MVP must account for:
duplicate listings
expired jobs
changing URLs
broken application links
inaccurate third-party listings
recruiter spam
outdated information
source reliability
The platform must respect applicable website terms, access restrictions, robots rules, licensing requirements and other legal constraints.

35. The Core MVP Hypothesis
The entire MVP should test this:
If Indian nurses can discover relevant jobs and government recruitment opportunities in one trustworthy place, understand the opportunity in simple language, see whether they are likely to qualify, and receive alerts when important information changes, they will return regularly.
If this is proven:
Then build:
Profile → Resume → Matching → Recommendations → AI → Career intelligence.

36. Hypotheses We Should NOT Test Yet

Will nurses learn German through us?

40. The Fundamental Differentiator
The product should eventually move from:
EXISTING MARKET
Information → Nurse
to:
YOUR PRODUCT

This is the central whitespace.

41. The MVP in One Sentence
Build a trusted, simple nursing opportunity layer that aggregates and structures government and private jobs, recruitment exams and important career updates, connects every opportunity to its official source, gives nurses basic personalized relevance based on qualification/experience/location, and alerts them when something important changes.

42. The Final Product Thesis
The thesis is not:
“Nurses need an app.”
The thesis is:
“Nurses already have recurring career-information needs, but today they solve them across fragmented sources. If we consolidate the most important opportunities, make them trustworthy and easy to understand, and personalize what matters to each nurse, we can become the place they return to for their next career action.”
The MVP therefore has one job:
Become the trusted place a nurse checks for what they can do next.
Everything else — resume matching, AI, global market intelligence, career recommendations, notes and classes — should be built only after this behaviour is proven.
