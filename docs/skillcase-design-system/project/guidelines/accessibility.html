Accessibility foundations

Phase 7. The 71 SkillCase components and representative production screens audited against eleven criteria before anything was changed. The system's lowest-scoring dimension, and the first phase in which a value had to be designed rather than read off the product.

Date 27 Aug 2026Components audited 71Contrast pairs measured 25New rules 4Components changed 4No screens redesigned

0The three findings

1. The components are better than expected, and the reason is luck. All 55 onClick handlers but four sit on real <button> elements, all 20 <img> carry alt, and — critically — not one component sets outline: none, so the browser's default focus ring has survived everywhere. Production does the opposite: outline-none appears on essentially every button. The system is accessible here because nobody removed the default, not because anybody specified one.

2. No single-colour focus ring can work in this product. Measured against the four surfaces a focused control actually sits on, brand blue fails on the gold CTA (2.34) and production's blue-600 fails on both gold and navy (2.84 / 2.83). This is not a preference to be settled by taste — a dual ring is the only thing that passes, and the measurements are below.

3. Three semantic colours fail AA, and one of them was introduced by this project last phase. --ui-text-faint is 2.56:1. It is used for counts and chevrons — including "12 of 20 correct" on results screens. They are faithful reproductions of production values, so they are staged with measured alternates rather than silently corrected.

--ui-danger-fg is the subtle one: 3.76 passes as a UI component or large text, so a red icon or a 20px heading is fine. It is used as 11px explanation text on review screens, where 4.5 applies. The value is not wrong; its usage is.

Not corrected — staged. Each alternate is the smallest darkening that clears 4.5:1 on both white and the sunken wash, published as --ui-text-faint-aa, --ui-warning-fg-aa and --ui-danger-fg-aa alongside the shipping values. Changing the app's appearance on the strength of a ratio is a product call (D-005, D-023); approving one is now a one-line swap rather than a colour exercise.

Two graphical-contrast failures worth naming separately

  • The block progress bar. Amber fill #fcd34d against zinc track #f4f4f5 is 1.31:1. Progress needs 3:1 against adjacent colours; filled and unfilled segments are close to indistinguishable. Present in eight surfaces.
  • The hand-rolled exam ring. #0BAA45 at 3.07 passes as a graphical object — but it renders green at every score (a P1 defect already), so its contrast is the least of that component's problems.

New system rules and tokens

Focus
--ui-focus-ring, dual, per theme. Never outline: none without a replacement.
Target size
--ui-target-min 44px — tokenised so the rule is checkable rather than remembered.
Motion
A prefers-reduced-motion block. Transitions are shortened, not removed — a 0ms transition can make a state change imperceptible, which is its own accessibility problem. Looping decorative animation stops outright; motion carrying state opts out with data-motion-essential.
Contrast
Three -aa alternates, staged.

Deliberately not done

  • No blanket ARIA sweep. Adding role and aria-label to components that are already real buttons with real text makes them worse, not better. ARIA was added in exactly one place, where the visible text ("Ch. 3") is not a usable name.
  • No live-region component. Announcing busy and error states is genuinely required, but it is a pattern spanning AppButton busy, SkeletonList and StateMessage — and the right shape depends on how screens compose them. Building it now would be guessing. Recorded as the top phase-8 input.
  • No contrast corrections applied. Staged, per above.
  • No target-size or text-size changes. Both are product decisions on faithfully-reproduced values.
  • No focus ring on an input. The checkpoint asked for one and there is no input to put it on: the only field component is the imported Untitled UI InputField sprite, not a SkillCase-authored control. Production already ships a real, consistent focus treatment for fields (focus:ring-2 focus:ring-blue-600) — text fields are the one control class that is not broken today. Recorded as open question 11 rather than forced.

5Defects raised

Engineering issues, in guidelines/defects.md. The system-side fixes above do not fix the product.

SeverityDefect
P1Focus is removed product-wide (outline-none, ~40 instances) with no replacement. The app is not keyboard-operable in any visible sense.
P2Selectable rows are clickable <div>s across ten-plus surfaces — not tabbable, not activatable by keyboard.
P2No prefers-reduced-motion handling anywhere, against six keyframes plus pulse and spin animations.
P2Block progress bar fill-to-track contrast is 1.31:1 — filled and unfilled segments are barely distinguishable. Eight surfaces.
P3Locked and disabled states are visual only; no aria-disabled, so assistive tech is told nothing.
P3Busy and error states are not announced — silence during submission and on failure.

6Maturity

Accessibility 2 → 5 / 10

What earned it. The foundations now exist and are validated rather than asserted: a focus rule with measurements behind it, applied to and checked on five real control surfaces across both themes; a target-size rule that is checkable; a motion rule justified by the animations actually shipping and corrected where the rule itself broke a component. Keyboard operability is correct in all 71 components.

What keeps it at 5. Three known contrast failures remain live by decision. Nothing has been tested with an actual screen reader. Busy and error states are still silent, which is a real barrier and not a small one. And every fix here is in the design system — the product is unchanged, and its P1 focus removal is untouched.

Overall system maturity 6.0 → 6.4. Accessibility 2→5; components 8 unchanged (the fixes corrected regressions rather than adding capability). The checkpoint did not raise the score: withdrawing three tokens and correcting a phantom colour makes the system more honest, not more capable.

Phase 7 is closed. What it genuinely established: one measured focus decision applied to five controls; one target rule; one motion rule with a stated exemption test; contrast measured across both themes with three remedies staged; and eight defects raised against the product. What remains provisional: nothing in the token set — the provisional entries were withdrawn rather than promoted. What remains unknown: screen-reader behaviour, which no amount of code reading settles.

7Open decisions

  1. Approve the three AA colour corrections? Measured and staged; they change the app's appearance slightly.
  2. Does AppButton sm stay at 36px? Either it grows to 44, or it is documented as non-touch-only.
  3. Do the 8px captions stay? Both are secondary to a number that is already large and legible — a defensible position, but it should be taken deliberately.
  4. Is a screen-reader pass in scope? Until one happens, that row stays unknown and no score above 6 is honest.

Highest-leverage phase 8 input: the announcement pattern for busy and error states. It spans three existing components and should be designed once, as a pattern, when templates are.
