# MayaBubble

Intentional addition (no Figma component set; built from the design file's frames). Maya plus her speech bubble — the guide's voice on onboarding, the journey and every lesson step.

```jsx
<MayaBubble text="What is your current German level?" image="/assets/img/maya/maya-wave-head.png" bubbleWidth={138} />
```

White bubble at radius 12, Poppins Medium 17 at 100% line-height, a 34 × 30.5 tail and a 0 4px 13px rgba(0,0,0,.25) drop. `side="right"` mirrors Maya and the tail. Pin `bubbleWidth` where the design does (138 in onboarding, 205 in lessons).
